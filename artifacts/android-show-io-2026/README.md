## Short Take

Google is positioning Android as an "intelligence system" rather than just a mobile operating system. The announcement cluster centers on Gemini Intelligence as an OS-level assistance layer, Android 17 platform and safety upgrades, Android Auto and car UI modernization, Googlebook as a Gemini-first laptop category, and the first explicit Android generative UI surface through custom widgets and Wear OS Tiles.
